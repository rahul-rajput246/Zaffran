Behind the scenes with our master chefs - preserving Indian culinary heritage.

  • 10 July, 2025
  • By: Admin
  • Awadhi Gastronomy

Elegance in Every Artful Bite

Dum Pukht is one of the most refined culinary methods ever developed in the Indian subcontinent. Originating in the royal kitchens of the Nawabs of Awadh, it involves cooking over very low heat in a heavy bottomed vessel sealed tightly with dough (a process called "Parda").

The Art of Awadhi Dum Cooking

The secret lies in the seal. As the vessel warms over slow-burning charcoal, the juices evaporate, rise as steam, condense on the sealed lid, and trickle back down through the meat and rice. This continuous self-basting cycle preserves every nuance of the 24 herbs in our secret potli masala, imparting an ethereal aroma and tenderness that high-heat cooking can never replicate.

“
The Alchemy of Potli Masala and Saffron Infusion
”

Hand-Pounded Potli Masala

The secret of authentic royal Awadhi cuisine lies in the Potli Masala—a muslin spice bouquet packed with over 24 rare botanical herbs, roots, and spices. Prepared by our master chefs each dawn, this aromatic pouch includes whole green and black cardamom, star anise, dried rose petals, mace, nutmeg, cinnamon quills, and vetiver root (khas ki jadd).

Unlike ground powders which can cloud the sauce, the muslin potli allows the warm broth to circulate freely, extracting delicate essential oils without bitter notes. The handi is then covered with heavy dough, ensuring that none of the heavenly aromatics escape. As the handi simmers for hours over dying coals, the meat softens to silk, absorbing the layered warmth of royal heritage.

"Dum cooking is not merely a cooking method; it is an act of royal meditation. When the seal is broken tableside at Zaffran, the wave of fragrant steam that envelops your senses is centuries of Indian history coming alive." — Executive Chef Vikramaditya Sharma

Purity of Kashmiri Saffron

  • Grade 1 Mongra Saffron sourced directly from certified heritage growers in Pampore, Kashmir.

  • Steeped in warm whole milk for four hours to extract deep golden color and sweet floral notes.

  • Layered into aged Himalayan basmati rice and signature gravies before sealing with dough.

  • 100% natural, unadulterated, and free from any synthetic colors or essences.
