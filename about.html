our story

Where Exquisite Flavors Meet Elegant Ambiance for an Unforgettable Fine Dining Experience!

At Zaffran, we celebrate the regal culinary legacy of ancient royal courts. Every morning begins with stone-grinding whole spices, infusing pure Kashmiri saffron, and lighting the live charcoal clay ovens. We honor the timeless philosophy of "Atithi Devo Bhava" (The Guest is Sacred), offering our diners an unhurried, royal dining experience where every flavor tells a story.
